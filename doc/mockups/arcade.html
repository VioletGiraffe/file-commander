Arcade

The design the Arcade theme is built to: a mock of the Qt window in HTML. JetBrains Mono stands in for the app's monospace font, and the glow is drawn with CSS shadows.

Where the app differs

  • The frame's glow shows only outside the frame; here it also lights the inside.
  • The folder summary reads 2/6 files, 0/3 folders selected (2.15 MiB / 9.61 MiB): every number is in the cursor colour, the slashes and the words are dim.
